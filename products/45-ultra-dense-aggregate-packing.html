DETAILS

Ultra-Dense Aggregate Packing

Generate ultra-dense graded aggregate systems with total aggregate volume fractions above 40%. Supports arbitrary aggregate shapes, optional ITZ, uniform generation or packed distributions, and user-defined size scaling and content.

  • Aggregate volume fraction above 40%
  • Cube or cylinder matrices
  • Arbitrary aggregate shapes including spheres, polyhedra, fibers and blocks
  • Custom volume fraction and size range for each aggregate type
  • Uniform or packed distribution modes
  • Optional ITZ
  • Exportable to ANSYS, CAD and COMSOL
