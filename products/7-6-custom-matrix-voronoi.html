DETAILS

Custom-Matrix 3D Voronoi

Generate 3D Voronoi grains directly inside user-defined matrix geometry. An iterative algorithm is used to optimize grain distribution and uniformity rather than creating a cube first and trimming it afterward.

  • Voronoi generation inside custom 3D geometry
  • Iterative grain-uniformity optimization
  • User control of randomness and uniformity
  • Optional grain boundaries with custom thickness
  • Supports user-specified seed coordinates
  • Suitable for Abaqus 2020 and later
