3D Random Sheet Cracks

Generate random 3D sheet-like cracks in a user-defined matrix. Crack shape, number and orientation range can be customized, and users can choose whether cracks are allowed to contact the outer matrix surface.

  • Custom crack quantity
  • Custom crack-angle range
