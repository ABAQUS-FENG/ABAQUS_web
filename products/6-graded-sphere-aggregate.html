DETAILS

3D Graded Sphere Aggregate

Generate multiple user-defined gradations of spherical aggregates directly in Abaqus. Particle size ranges and proportions can be entered in tables, ITZ can be enabled or disabled, and the aggregates can be generated inside arbitrary matrix shapes.

  • Arbitrary number of aggregate gradations
  • Custom particle sizes and proportions
  • Optional ITZ with custom thickness
  • Supports cube, cylinder, sphere and custom matrices
  • Includes Fuller gradation calculator
  • Exportable to COMSOL, CAD and ANSYS
