DETAILS

Aggregate + Fiber Generator

Generate pure aggregates, pure fibers, or mixed aggregate-fiber models in one workflow. Multiple aggregate gradations and multiple fiber size ranges are supported, together with ITZ options and fiber orientation control.

  • Spherical or polyhedral aggregates with multiple size ranges
  • Optional ITZ for aggregate families
  • Solid cylindrical fibers or line fibers
  • Multiple fiber size ranges
  • Custom fiber orientation range and directional fibers
  • Custom line-fiber shapes including hooked-end fibers
  • One-click mixed aggregate-fiber model generation
  • Exportable to COMSOL and ANSYS
